# Business Plan & Technical Feasibility Report: Shrijee Aqua Enterprises
**Location:** Sarsawa (near Sadpur / Saharanpur region), Uttar Pradesh — PIN: 247232  
**Industry:** Commercial RO Water Purification & Packaging Plant / 20L Camper & Bottled Water Distribution  

---

### 1. Executive Summary & Local Context
- **Business Entity:** Shrijee Aqua Enterprises
- **Core Operations:**
  1. **Bulk Commercial Water Distribution (Campers):** 20-liter food-grade reusable insulated campers for local commercial hubs, offices, dhabas/eateries, wedding halls, and households in and around Sarsawa & Sadpur. Target pilot run: 50 to 100 campers daily.
  2. **Packaged Drinking Water & Small Bottle Filling:** Filling and sealing units for 250 ml, 500 ml, and 1000 ml (1 Liter) PET bottles catering to highway retail, rural transit points, and local retail stores.
- **Geographic & Ground Water Dynamics (PIN 247232 - Sarsawa/Saharanpur):**
  - Sarsawa sits in the fertile Upper Doab region (Yamuna-Hindustan plains). Groundwater typically ranges between 350 to 650 ppm Total Dissolved Solids (TDS), often with elevated seasonal hardness, turbidity, and agricultural runoff (nitrates).
  - Target purified water specs: TDS 80–120 ppm, neutral pH 7.2–7.6, remineralized with essential calcium/magnesium, UV treated, and ozonated.

---

### 2. Plant Configuration & Technical Machinery Setup
1. **Raw Water Sourcing & Pre-Treatment (1000 LPH Capacity):**
   - **Borewell Feed Pump:** 1.5 HP Multistage / Submersible stainless steel pump.
   - **Dual Media Filter (DMF / Sand Filter):** Removes suspended particles, silt, and turbidity down to 20–30 microns.
   - **Activated Carbon Filter (ACF):** High iodine-value activated carbon bed to strip odor, taste, chlorine, pesticides, and organic matter.
   - **Dosing System:** Antiscalant dosing pump to protect membrane longevity.
   - **Micron Cartridge Filters:** 5 micron and 1 micron SS housing filters for pre-RO membrane security.

2. **Main RO Module (1000 LPH / 1 m³/hr):**
   - **High-Pressure Pump:** CRI or Grundfos vertical multistage SS pump.
   - **Membranes:** High rejection TFC polyamide membranes (4040 size, 4–5 units).
   - **Instrumentation:** Flow meters (permeate & reject), digital TDS meter, high/low pressure switches, SS skid structure.

3. **Post-Treatment & Sterilization:**
   - **Mineral Addition / Alkaline Filter:** Injection of food-grade calcium and magnesium salts for taste profile.
   - **Industrial UV Sterilizer:** 1000 LPH SS-304/316 chamber with quartz sleeve.
   - **Ozone Generator:** 2 to 5 g/hr corona discharge unit with recirculating venturi for disinfection and shelf-life extension in 20L campers and bottles.

4. **Filling Lines & Packaging Equipment:**
   - **Automatic/Semi-Automatic Bottle Filling Machine (18 BPM - Bottles Per Minute):**
     - Rinsing, Filling, and Capping 3-in-1 rotary/linear unit suitable for 250 ml, 500 ml, and 1 L PET bottles.
     - Heat shrink tunnel or sleeve labeling station.
     - Date & Batch Coding inkjet machine.
   - **20L Camper Washing & Manual/Semi-Auto 2-Head Filling Station:**
     - High pressure internal jet rinsing station.
     - Fast fill nozzle with overflow catch.
     - Manual press cap/tamper-evident sealing band applicator.

### 4. Operational Unit Economics & Market Strategy (Sarsawa & Sadpur)
- **20L Camper Model:**
  - Production & Filling Cost (Electricity + Water + Cap + Delivery): ~₹6 to ₹8 per camper.
  - Retail/Wholesale Delivery Price in local market: ₹25 to ₹40 per camper.
  - Gross Margin per camper: ₹18 to ₹32. With 80 campers/day = ₹1,500 – ₹2,400 daily margin (~₹45,000–₹70,000/month).
- **PET Bottles (250ml, 500ml, 1L):**
  - 1L bottle production cost: ~₹4.50 (Preform ₹2.80 + Cap ₹0.45 + Label ₹0.50 + RO Water & Power ₹0.75).
  - Bulk supply to local retail dhabas & shops: ₹8 to ₹10 per bottle (MRP ₹15 to ₹20).
- **Distribution Radius:** 5–10 km radius covering Sarsawa highway corridor, Sadpur market, petrol pumps, local schools, and weekly bazaars using an e-rickshaw or mini cargo pickup.
