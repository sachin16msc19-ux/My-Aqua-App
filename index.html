Quality assurance and BIS compliance

Every drop of Guru Jal goes through multi-barrier purification before it reaches customers in Sarsawa and Sandpur. Standards: FSSAI and IS 14543.

Five-step purification pipeline

  1. Raw water storage and pre-settling. Sourced from verified groundwater in the Sarsawa region.
  2. Dual media and activated carbon filter. Removes turbidity, suspended solids, colour and organic odours.
  3. 100 LPH high-pressure reverse osmosis. Brings dissolved solids (TDS) to a drinking range of 100 to 150 mg/L.
  4. Ultraviolet disinfection. Neutralizes microbiological pathogens.
  5. Ozonation and 0.2 micron micro-filtration. Gives long shelf life in PET bottles and prevents bacterial regrowth in 20L campers.
