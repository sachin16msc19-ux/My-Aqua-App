## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, pristine aesthetic tailored for regional pure water supply, commercial camper logistics, and domestic reverse osmosis (RO) systems. The brand persona balances industrial-grade sanitation with modern household convenience: clinically pure, reliable, crisp, and refreshing.

The visual direction combines **Minimalism** with subtle **Translucent Fluidity**. The design communicates clinical water safety without feeling cold or pharmaceutical; instead, it evokes mineral clarity, cool hydration, and prompt doorstep reliability across Western Uttar Pradesh. Clean geometry, generous whitespace, pristine micro-borders, and liquid-light gradients convey premium reliability for residential consumers, commercial offices, and regional events.

## Colors

The color palette translates the natural states of pure water under sunlight into a coherent digital identity:

- **Primary (`#0A2540` - Deep Trench Navy):** Anchors high-trust structural layers, typography, primary buttons, and navigational surfaces. It conveys authority, certified mineral quality, and corporate dependability.
- **Secondary (`#00B4D8` - Vivid Cyan Wave):** Delivers vitality to key call-to-actions, active filter alerts, scheduled delivery trackers, and water freshness badges.
- **Tertiary (`#72EFDD` - Crystal Aqua Mist):** Serves as an atmospheric accent for subtle gradient fills, highlights, interactive toggles, and status icons indicating completed purifications or TDS safety ranges.
- **Neutral (`#F4F9FC` - Glacial Off-White):** Forms the ambient canvas. It eliminates harsh clinical white in favor of a soft, mineralized backdrop that allows aqua elements to glow effortlessly.

Use pure white (`#FFFFFF`) strictly as the elevated card surface, surrounded by `#E1EDF5` for crisp hair-line containment. Reserve high-contrast deep navy for all actionable microcopy to guarantee effortless outdoor readability in bright daylight.

## Typography

The typography system employs a dual-typeface strategy engineered for technical precision and warm local approachability:

- **Headlines & Technical Labels (Space Grotesk):** Provides engineering rigor, purity metrics, water delivery capacities (e.g., 20L Camper, TDS < 80), and prominent page headings. Its geometric, slightly technical cuts reflect scientific purification standards and mechanized plant consistency.
- **Body Text & Form Data (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Balances technical headings with humanized, approachable warmth. Its open counters and clean geometry ensure effortless legibility when reading service plans, billing breakdowns, or order tracking on budget mobile screens under harsh sunlight.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system is built upon a 12-column responsive grid on desktop devices (breakpoint: `1024px+`), collapsing into an 8-column layout for tablets (`768px - 1023px`), and a 4-column flow on mobile viewports (`<768px`).

Content aligns against an 8px base rhythm. Outer section vertical padding utilizes expansive increments (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`) to preserve the airy, unpolluted atmosphere essential to the brand. Element alignments prioritize high-density data presentation for order manifests and booking inputs, while marketing sections (water purity tests, filtration steps, delivery radius) leverage relaxed spacing to maximize focus and visual calm.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy, drop-shadow opacity. Instead, depth is achieved through **pristine surface layers, subtle aquatic refraction, and ambient water-tinted glow**:

- **Level 0 (Basal Ground):** Base neutral floor (`#F4F9FC`). Completely flat.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Pure white solid base with a 1px border colored `#E1EDF5`. Shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Modules & Modals):** Semi-translucent glass effect with backdrop blur (12px). Surface: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` backed by `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.12)` with a 1px perimeter outline in `rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.25)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Booking Bar & Floating Delivery CTA):** High-priority controls utilize a localized ambient cyan glow: `0 8px 24px -2px rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework uses a balanced **Rounded (`2`)** approach to mirror soft water droplets enclosed in clean modern architecture:

- Standard structural panels, card wrappers, and input boxes adopt a default `0.5rem` (`8px`) radius.
- Large service highlight cards and feature modules use `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`).
- Deep interactive targets—such as delivery quantity steppers, filter cartridge status chips, and quick order pills—elevate to full `rounded-full` boundaries to evoke liquid droplets and tactile smoothness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Instant Delivery / Book Service):** Deep Navy background (`#0A2540`), crisp white Space Grotesk text, `rounded-md` (8px), padding `12px 24px`. On hover, transitions effortlessly to Cyan Wave (`#00B4D8`) with an ambient cyan glow.
- **Secondary (Water Quality / Pricing Details):** Clear background with 1.5px border in `#00B4D8`, text in `#0A2540`. On hover, fills with light tint `rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.08)`.
- **Quick-Order Floating Button:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), Cyan background (`#00B4D8`), high contrast navy icon and label, sticky to mobile screen bottom.

### Chips & Badges
- **Purity & Safety Indicators (e.g., "7-Stage RO", "TDS 65 Verified"):** Pure white surface, 1px border in `#72EFDD`, text in `#0A2540`. Prefixed with a vibrant cyan pulsing dot.
- **Stock & Area Coverage (e.g., "Delivering in Sarsawa"):** Subtle emerald/aqua fill (`rgba(114, 239, 221, 0.2)`), text in `#0A2540`.

### Cards
- **Product Cards (20L Pure Camper, Domestic RO, Chilled Dispensers):** Pristine white surface, 1px border `#E1EDF5`. Header showcases volume capacity in Space Grotesk. Incorporates a dynamic order counter (+ / -) directly integrated into the footer.
- **Service & AMC Tracking Cards:** Clean grid layouts displaying upcoming filter replacement dates, water hardness metrics, and technician dispatch timelines with clear directional arrows.

### Input Fields & Steppers
- **Camper Quantity Counter:** Pill-shaped enclosure containing large bold numbers flanked by tactile minus/plus circular touch zones.
- **Location & Phone Input:** Solid white canvas, 1px border `#CBDDE9`, labeled cleanly with `label-md`. On focus, border transitions to `#00B4D8` accompanied by a 3px ring in `rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Toggles
- Custom aquatic rounded indicators. When checked, container fills with `#00B4D8` displaying an ultra-clean white checkmark icon; unselected states maintain a 1.5px border in `#CBDDE9` against pure white.

### Specialized Water Quality Meter
- A horizontal dynamic gauge component visualizing real-time purification levels (TDS, pH balance, Mineral infusion) utilizing a graduated gradient bar shifting smoothly from `#72EFDD` to `#00B4D8` with numeric markers in Space Grotesk.
